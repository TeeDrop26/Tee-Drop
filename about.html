About PlayTeeDrop

Locally owned and operated in Tuscarawas County.

PlayTeeDrop is an Ohio public-golf directory, locally owned and operated in Tuscarawas County.

Tee Drop brings public golf course links, booking options, phone information, and posted rates together in one straightforward directory. The goal is to save golfers time while sending them directly to the courses they choose.

PlayTeeDrop independently maintains its course information and website. Community members may share or recommend the site, but sharing it does not indicate ownership, authorship, or affiliation.

PlayTeeDrop does not process bookings or payments. Golfers book directly with the listed course and should confirm current rates and availability before playing.
